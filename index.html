On the syllabus

Algebra, Geometry & Calculus

Limits, derivatives, and the area hiding under a curve.

Read more

I love doing mathematics. As an ISC student I rarely come across difficult questions, so I regularly go beyond my syllabus and extend my knowledge using books such as Mathematical Circles and Higher Algebra by Hall and Knight. Using these books I took the IOQM — the Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics — and scored in the top 10% both nationwide and statewide.

I furthered my studies in calculus using Thomas' Calculus. It was a tremendously useful resource and helped me achieve a 4 in Advanced Placement (AP) Calculus BC.

On the syllabus

Physics

Forces, motion, and everything Newton started.

Read more

I am extremely passionate about physics. I love reading articles about scientists pushing the frontiers and boundaries of what is possible. I try to push myself using amazing online resources from Walter Lewin and books like Resnick and Halliday and Kleppner and Kolenkow. Through striving and pushing myself I achieved a 5 in Advanced Placement (AP) Physics 1 and a 4 in AP Physics C: Mechanics.
