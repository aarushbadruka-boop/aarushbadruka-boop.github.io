Research Green technology

Turning air-conditioner exhaust into power

Abstract. The increasing demand for air conditioners raises the problem of thermal energy wastage. Domestic air conditioners waste low-grade heat of 30–70 °C, which cannot be used efficiently by classical heat-to-power methods. This paper presents an approach to the sustainable conversion of this heat energy into usable electrons, stored in an electric cell.

Experimental research has been performed on two different materials, stainless-steel and copper plates, with thermal adhesive to optimize physical conduction into a Peltier module. The temperature difference between the two sides of the module creates the need for an optimal cold-side heat sink to maximize domestic on-site power generation.

The developed algorithms are applied to real-time data, and the outcomes are compared to identify the better efficiency, as proved through data visualization and graphical representation.

Paper
Design and Development of a Device to Harvest AC Compressor Exhaust Heat for Sustainable Electrical Energy Production
Field
Green technology, energy recovery
With
Dr. Om Prakash Sahu (UPES, Dehradun), Khyati Verma & Lekha Sahu (SSIPMT, Raipur)
